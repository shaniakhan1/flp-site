FLP Marketing Group is a marketing and public relations agency founded in Houston by Shania Khan in 2018. We help businesses tell their story through social media, brand strategy, PR, email, and search.

One agency. A few ways you may know us.

FLP stands for For the Love of People. You may also see us listed as FLP Group or For The Love Of People Group. These names refer to the same agency.

What we help with

  • Social media management: strategy, content planning, creative, publishing, and performance review.
  • Brand strategy and public relations: positioning, messaging, story development, media outreach, and creator partnerships.
  • Email marketing and funnels: newsletters, welcome sequences, lead magnets, and landing-page messaging.
  • Search and local visibility: website content, business information, and documented visibility checks.

Every engagement has an agreed scope. We confirm deliverables, approvals, timing, and the full price before work begins. Social media management starts at $4,000/month; other services are quoted separately.
