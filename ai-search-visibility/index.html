WHAT WE DO

AI search and local business visibility

Help customers understand what you do, where you work, and how to take the next step. FLP improves the business information on your website and local profiles.

FLP Marketing Group · Updated September 19, 2026

Who this is for

Established service businesses with an existing website and capacity to respond to new inquiries. Based in Houston. Website compatibility and business fit are confirmed before an engagement begins.

What you receive

  • A website and business-profile review with specific, documented findings
  • A baseline for ten agreed buyer questions, with the search surface, location, and date recorded
  • One business-profile update and up to five listing corrections, where access allows
  • One existing service-page update, with your approval of the facts and scope
  • A review-request template and supported workflow
  • Inquiry measurement setup, with completed work reported separately from business results

How it works

Start with a free service-page draft using your own business details. Review the copy and download it, or copy it into your existing website. If you want FLP to implement a wider set of improvements, send your visibility brief. We confirm access, website compatibility, delivery availability, scope, and costs before any paid work begins.

Before we start

The free draft builder does not research your business or publish to your website. Managed implementation is a separate engagement covering one location, one priority service, and an existing website. New websites, multiple locations, advertising, and custom integrations require a separate scope. FLP does not guarantee rankings, AI recommendations, traffic, inquiries, or sales. You approve factual claims before changes are made.
