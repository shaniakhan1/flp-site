Questions about AI search

How do I optimize my business for AI search?

Start with a clear page for your actual service, a working contact path, accurate business profiles, and examples customers can verify. Check whether search engines can access and index the important pages. Then record how your business appears for relevant buyer questions and whether those visits become qualified inquiries.

What do AI SEO, GEO, and AEO mean?

AI SEO usually means improving visibility in AI-assisted search. GEO stands for generative engine optimization; AEO stands for answer engine optimization. The terms overlap. For Google’s AI search features, established SEO practices still apply: accessible pages, useful information, and evidence behind your claims.

Does my website matter more than my Google Business Profile?

They do different jobs. Your website explains your services, experience, process, and next steps. Your business profile supplies local details such as your category, service area, hours, and reviews. Keep both accurate so customers can understand the same business wherever they find it.

Do I have to let AI companies train on my website?

Search access and model training are separate choices at OpenAI. OAI-SearchBot supports ChatGPT search; GPTBot concerns content that may be used for model training. You can allow the search crawler while disallowing the training crawler. Hosting controls also affect access, and allowing a crawler does not guarantee a recommendation.

How do we know whether the work is helping?

Separate visibility from business results. A recorded answer can show whether your business was named or a page was cited at that moment. Referrals, qualified inquiries, appointments, and won work answer different questions. We record the search surface, location, and date for the agreed baseline and report completed work separately from results. One favorable answer is not a stable ranking.
