AI Search Visibility for Service Businesses

A managed service for improving the information people and search platforms use to understand your business.

FLP Marketing Group · Updated September 19, 2026

What does AI search visibility mean?

AI search visibility describes whether and how a business appears when someone asks an AI-enabled search tool a relevant question. It is different from being indexed, getting a website visit, receiving an inquiry, or winning a customer. We measure those separately.

FLP combines website and local-profile improvements with defined checks of search answers. The standard engagement covers one location, one priority service, and an existing website. Our initial focus is established home-service businesses; other categories are assessed for fit.

What FLP handles

We review the website and business profile, compare relevant competitors, establish a baseline for ten agreed buyer questions, and prioritize the information gaps we can substantiate. Implementation covers one business profile, up to five listing corrections, one existing service page, a review-request template and supported workflow, and inquiry measurement setup. You approve factual claims and access before changes are made.

A Houston HVAC example

For a business offering AC repair, a useful review would check its actual service area, contact options, operating hours, documented qualifications, and whether the service page answers a customer's buying questions. It would compare the information on the website and business profile, then record which businesses and sources appear for a defined set of Houston-area questions. This is an example of the method, not a client result.

A business operating only during weekday hours should not be positioned as an emergency 24-hour provider. Improving visibility starts with a clear and accurate description of the service the business can actually deliver.

How much does it cost?

The first 30-day setup is $2,000, subject to access, website compatibility, and confirmed delivery availability. Optional ongoing care is $1,500 per month under a separate agreement. External software, paid listings, and usage charges are additional and agreed before purchase. See the full scope and exclusions.

What makes the work reviewable?

Each task records the observed issue, supporting source, proposed change, approval, and completion evidence. Visibility checks keep the question, location, surface, and date. Reporting separates work delivered from results observed. Read our measurement method.

What cannot be promised?

FLP does not guarantee ranking, AI recommendations, traffic, inquiries, or sales. Google states that its existing SEO fundamentals remain relevant to AI Overviews and AI Mode, with no special additional markup required. OpenAI states that placement in ChatGPT search is not guaranteed. Read our guide to the platform requirements.

Who is it a fit for?

An established business with a real service, satisfied customers, an existing website, and capacity to respond to new inquiries is a better fit than a business still deciding what it sells. Multiple locations, new-site builds, advertising, and custom integrations require a separate scope.
