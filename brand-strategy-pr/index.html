Make the story worth sharing.

Your history, your point of view, and the detail customers remember can give your business a story people want to repeat. FLP helps you find that story and give it direction.

Brand strategy starts with the business

We look at who you serve, what they need, and why they choose you. That work informs positioning, messaging, content themes, and campaign ideas. It should help your business communicate consistently across its website, social channels, email, and outreach.

PR and creator partnerships

A relevant pitch gives an editor or creator a reason to pay attention. We develop story angles, identify suitable outreach opportunities, and prepare messaging around the agreed campaign. Creator partnerships and local tastemaker outreach can be scoped alongside media relations.

  • Brand positioning and messaging
  • Campaign concepts and story development
  • Media-outreach strategy and pitching within an agreed scope
  • Creator-partnership planning and local outreach

What makes an engagement specific?

We agree on the audience, campaign objective, deliverables, outreach scope, approval process, and reporting. A brand-positioning project is different from a recurring media campaign. Creator fees, production, travel, and paid placements are separate costs when applicable.

A real story needs real evidence

You provide accurate business history, product or service details, approved assets, and any results we can substantiate. We shape the story without inventing customer experiences or achievements. Media coverage, creator participation, and resulting sales are never guaranteed.
