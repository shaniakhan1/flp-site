FLP MARKETING GROUP · HOUSTON · EST. 2018

Give your audience a reason to come back.

A follow is the beginning of a relationship. Email gives you a way to continue it with people who have asked to hear from you.

Email marketing built around the next step

We start with what your audience needs and what your business offers. A local business may need a useful newsletter and an inquiry path. A creator may need a welcome sequence connecting a free resource to a paid product. The sequence should make sense to the reader before it becomes an automation.

  • Email list-growth strategy and lead-magnet planning
  • Welcome sequences and campaign copy
  • Newsletters and Substack content planning
  • Landing-page messaging and sales-funnel planning
  • Supported workflow setup and review, as agreed in scope

New build or improvement?

For an existing system, we review the signup experience, message sequence, offer, and destination page. For a new build, we first confirm your tools, required integrations, content, and approval process. These are different projects with different costs.

What you provide

Your offer, brand assets, permission-based subscriber information, and access to the agreed email platform. We confirm who owns the list, what subscribers agreed to receive, and how unsubscribe requests are handled. You retain your business accounts.

How we judge the work

We agree on relevant measures such as signups, clicks, qualified inquiries, or purchases where tracking is available. We distinguish activity from business results and check the customer journey before launch. Revenue depends on the offer, audience, traffic, and sales process as well as the emails.

Pricing and scope

Email and funnel projects are individually quoted. Message count, platform compatibility, landing-page work, and integration needs determine the scope. Software and usage charges are identified separately. These services are not automatically included in the $4,000/month social media starting engagement.

Pair the work with social media management or brand messaging when those are the gaps your business needs to address.
