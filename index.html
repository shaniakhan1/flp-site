BEFORE YOU DECIDE

The details matter.

What exactly is a funnel?

Here, it means a connected path: a page about one service, a way to inquire or request a booking, a confirmation, and an email follow-up sequence. It is not a full website rebuild or a promise of new demand.

Is this right if I don't have customers yet?

Usually not. The starting offer is for a business with a proven service and an existing source of visitors or inquiries. If you need advertising, demand research, or a new business model, that is different work.

How quickly will it be ready?

The proposed pilot target is a first draft within 10 business days after a complete brief and required access. This target must be validated in pilot delivery. A final launch date depends on your approval and successful connection tests.

Will it work with my existing tools?

The standard launch platform and supported connections will be published before checkout opens. The first package will use one supported email system and a booking link. Data migrations and custom integrations are outside scope.

What's outside the package?

Daily social content, ads and ad spend, ongoing PR, Google review management, multi-offer funnels, ecommerce checkout, custom dashboards, CRM migrations, phone or SMS agents, and unlimited revisions. The standard funnel has a fixed scope. Private advisory and implementation are scoped separately.

What changes are included each month?

One consolidated request: update the copy or assets in one existing page section, or update up to two emails in the existing sequence. A new page, offer, or sequence is a separate project. Unused requests do not accumulate.

Does AI talk to my customers on its own?

The pilot sends only approved emails. A real reply goes to your business contact. The system must stop reminders on replies, opt-outs, and connected booking signals before it launches. It does not invent prices or negotiate with customers.

How is private advisory different from the funnel package?

The funnel is one standard package for one offer. Private advisory and implementation start at $10,000/month for new engagements, with priorities, deliverables, access, and timing agreed individually. The starting price is not a promise of unlimited services or personal availability.

Do these offers change my existing FLP engagement?

No. Existing clients continue under their agreed scope and pricing. Published starting prices apply to new private engagements. Any change to an existing engagement is discussed and agreed directly.

Can I hire FLP for ongoing social media posting?

We are not taking on new social media posting engagements. Our new offers focus on a defined funnel or private advisory and implementation. Existing client work continues under its agreed scope.

Can I cancel the funnel plan, and who owns the work?

The proposed plan allows cancellation before the next monthly renewal. You retain the paid-for page, copy, and your customer data. Handover, hosting, software costs, and access removal will be defined in the service agreement before payment.
