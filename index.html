A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear before we start.

Is this right for my business?

The package is for a business with a proven service and an existing source of visitors or inquiries. If you need to find customers first, an acquisition plan comes before this setup.

How soon can we start?

Email us to check setup availability. We confirm the supported tools, start date, and delivery milestones before you commit. Launch follows your approval and successful connection tests.

Will it work with my existing tools?

We check your email system and booking calendar before accepting the project. Required software and usage costs are quoted separately. Custom integrations and data migrations are outside the standard scope.

What does monthly management include?

Form and follow-up checks, in-scope fixes, an activity summary, and one consolidated change request. That change can update one existing page section or up to two emails in the existing sequence. New pages, offers, and sequences are separate projects. Unused requests do not accumulate.

Does AI talk to customers on its own?

The sequence uses only emails you approve. Real replies go to your business contact. Reply, opt-out, and connected booking stop rules are tested before launch. The system does not invent prices or negotiate with customers.

What is outside the package?

Daily social content, ads and ad spend, ongoing PR, Google review management, multiple offers, ecommerce checkout, custom dashboards, CRM migrations, phone or SMS agents, and unlimited revisions. Private engagements are scoped separately.

What happens if I cancel?

The proposed terms allow cancellation before the next monthly renewal. Ownership of paid-for work and customer data, software costs, hosting, handover, and access removal will be set out in your agreement before payment.

Does this change an existing FLP engagement?

No. Existing clients continue under their agreed scope and pricing. Published prices apply to new engagements. Changes to existing work are discussed and agreed directly.
