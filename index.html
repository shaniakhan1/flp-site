CLEAR BEFORE WE START

The questions we’d ask, too.

Can you make ChatGPT recommend my business?

We can improve access to accurate, useful information and check how your business appears in a defined set of answers. No agency controls recommendations. Results vary by question, platform, location, and time. Read how we measure visibility.

Is the brief builder scanning my website?

No. It organizes the answers you provide into an initial plan. A real audit requires checking your website, business profiles, search results, and supporting data. The tool clearly separates those checks from your self-reported information.

What happens after I build my brief?

You can copy or download it, or open an email addressed to FLP. You decide whether to send. We confirm fit, access, delivery capacity, costs, and terms before any payment or work starts. There is no automatic account creation or subscription.

How quickly will we get more customers?

There is no guaranteed timeline or volume. The setup has defined deliverables, not a promised number of customers. We establish a baseline and monitor qualified inquiries separately from visibility. Businesses needing immediate demand may also need a separately scoped acquisition channel.

Does this work if I don’t have a website?

The standard setup improves an existing website. If you need a new site, multiple locations, or custom integrations, we assess that separately before agreeing to work together.
