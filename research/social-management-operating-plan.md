# FLP social management: proposed scope and delivery plan

Internal draft for founder review, September 29, 2026. This file is excluded from the public website. Existing client agreements do not change. These deliverable counts and staffing assumptions are recommendations, not a published package or a signed client commitment.

## Proposed $4,000/month base scope

- One business, one approval contact, two agreed social channels.
- Twelve original content pieces/month: six short videos up to 60 seconds edited from client-supplied footage, and six static/carousel pieces (up to five slides each).
- Adaptation of those twelve originals for the two agreed channels, up to 24 placements total. Adaptations are not another 12 original ideas.
- Monthly themes/calendar, captions, scheduling, and a concise performance summary.
- One consolidated revision round per monthly batch. One 30-minute planning/review call.
- Client provides approved brand assets, accurate claims and up to 30 minutes of organized usable raw footage/month.
- No filming, travel, paid media/ad spend, daily Stories, inbox replies, moderation, influencer fees, PR pitching, long-form YouTube, newsletters, or funnel builds included. Quote those separately.
- Turnaround and approval windows belong in the actual proposal after reviewing capacity. Late access/assets/feedback move the schedule; silence is not approval.
- Do not offer unlimited revisions or promise follower, lead, revenue, AI recommendation, or ranking targets.

Status flow: New inquiry → Fit review → Proposal → Agreement/payment → Access/assets ready → Draft → Client review → Approved → Scheduled → Reported. Do not automate acceptance or promise a start date without a capacity check.

## First 30 days

Week 1: agreement/payment confirmed, client access invitations, assets, business/voice brief, channel review.
Week 2: themes and first batch; founder quality review.
Week 3: consolidated client feedback, revisions, explicit approval, schedule.
Week 4: early performance/process review, next batch priorities. Actual publishing dates depend on assets and approvals.
