FLP MARKETING GROUP · HOUSTON · EST. 2018

Social media management that sounds like your business.

Your social presence should make it easier to understand, trust, and choose your business. FLP connects your brand story with a practical content plan and ongoing management.

Social media management starts at $4,000/month. The final scope depends on your channels, content volume, production needs, and community-management requirements.

What FLP manages

We begin with your audience, your offer, and the reason someone would choose you. From there, we develop content themes, plan the calendar, create agreed assets, write captions, and schedule approved work. Performance review helps us decide what to repeat, refine, or stop.

  • Social strategy, content direction, and a consistent brand voice
  • Content calendars, captions, publishing, and agreed reporting
  • Short-form video planning and editing from supplied footage
  • Community management when included in your agreed scope

The right channels for your audience

Our capabilities cover Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube, including short-form and long-form content. We select the channels around your audience and the resources available to keep them useful. Every channel is not automatically included in the starting price.

What happens before the first post?

We agree on channels, deliverable counts, formats, revision rounds, deadlines, and who approves the work. You provide access, brand materials, accurate business details, and the agreed photography or footage. Original filming and travel require a separate production scope.

What is separate from the monthly management fee?

Paid advertising, creator fees, PR campaigns, email marketing, and new funnel builds are separately quoted. Any setup costs and minimum term are disclosed before you commit. There is no guaranteed follower count, reach, or sales volume.
