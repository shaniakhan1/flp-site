Which social platforms does FLP manage?

Our capabilities cover Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube, including short-form and long-form content. We select the channels around your audience and the resources available to keep them useful. Every channel is not automatically included in the starting price.

What happens before the first post?

We agree on channels, deliverable counts, formats, revision rounds, deadlines, and who approves the work. You provide access, brand materials, accurate business details, and the agreed photography or footage. Original filming and travel require a separate production scope. See our first-month process and what we need from your team.

What is separate from the monthly management fee?

Paid advertising, creator fees, PR campaigns, email marketing, and new funnel builds are separately quoted. Any setup costs and minimum term are disclosed before you commit. There is no guaranteed follower count, reach, or sales volume.

How do I choose a social media agency in Houston?

Start with the work you need: a content strategy, ongoing publishing, filming, community management, or paid advertising. Ask to see relevant creative examples and the agency’s exact role in each project. Then compare the proposed channels, monthly deliverables, approval process, production costs, reporting, and contract terms.

For FLP, the examples above show community-focused brand content and founder-led editorial work. We confirm your fit, scope, and availability before you commit. Meet the agency and its founder.

How should social media performance be measured?

Choose measures that match the goal. Reach can show distribution; saves, shares, and substantive comments can help explain what people find useful. Inquiries, bookings, or purchases need appropriate tracking before they can be connected to social activity. A follower count alone does not establish a business result. We agree on reporting in your scope and distinguish observed activity from results the available data cannot prove.

Can social content support search and AI discovery?

Clear, consistent information across your website and public profiles helps people understand what your business does. Social content is one part of that picture. AI search optimization and local visibility address separate website and business-profile work, while brand strategy and PR help define and communicate your story. These services are scoped separately; social posting does not guarantee search rankings or AI recommendations.
