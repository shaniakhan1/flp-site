OUR BUSINESS GOES FIRST · SEPTEMBER 19, 2026

What happens when
we check ourselves?

FLP has been around since 2018. Our public information hasn’t always told the same story. Here’s what we observed and what needs to happen next.

This is an initial public-information check, not a completed AI recommendation audit. No visibility score or customer-growth claim is made.

Observed · company profile

Our history is visible.

Our LinkedIn company page identifies Houston as our headquarters and 2018 as our founding year. It describes PR and influence services. That is useful identity information, but the description needs to reflect our current offer.

Source: FLP’s LinkedIn company page ↗

Observed · different versions surfaced

Our website story needs to catch up in search.

During this check, a search result described FLP as a private consultancy. The search retrieval of our homepage returned an older lead-follow-up offer. A direct browser visit showed the current visibility offer.

These observations suggest older versions are still surfacing. They do not establish what every searcher sees or why the difference occurred.

Source: current FLP homepage ↗

Not measured

AI recommendations are still unverified.

We have not completed a controlled test of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Google’s AI answers. Broader web-search attempts returned results too unrelated to support a reliable buyer-question baseline. We excluded them rather than count them as “not found.”

Our next three actions.

  1. Make the story consistent. The homepage now includes our 2018 founding year. Align the company-profile description with the current offer after account access and approval.
  2. Verify indexing. Check the current homepage and service-page URLs in Search Console, review indexing issues, and request recrawling where appropriate. Account access is required; this has not been completed.
  3. Measure actual answers. Run the same five buyer questions on each agreed AI surface. Save dates, full answers, source links, and whether FLP is mentioned. Repeat before making any trend claim.
The five questions for the next check
  1. Which Houston agencies help service businesses improve AI search visibility?
  2. Who can help a Houston business get found on Google and AI search?
  3. Which agencies offer done-for-you local visibility work with public pricing?
  4. Who can improve a service business’s listings, website information, and customer proof?
  5. What does FLP Marketing Group do, and who is it for?

Proposed test questions, not verified search-volume data. The last is a branded identity check; the first four test discovery. Results must be recorded separately.

What this check can tell us.

It identifies specific information to verify and improve. It does not prove that changes will cause AI recommendations, rankings, leads, or sales. Those outcomes need separate measurement.
