DHARSH · PERSONAL BRAND & CONTENT STRATEGY

Give the person behind the business a voice.

Dharsh’s content starts with an operator’s perspective: standards, judgment, leadership, and the things running a business teaches you about people.

ClientDharsh
Account@CannaDharsh
FocusPersonal-brand content

The brief

Translate a business owner’s point of view into content people can recognize and relate to. Leadership becomes more interesting when the subject is a decision, a difficult conversation, or a standard someone refuses to lower.

The editorial decision

Build around specific observations. Put the central idea in the opening line, then give it context. Keep the language direct and grounded in the operator’s perspective, with room for personal moments alongside leadership content.

The content playbook separates three editorial themes so each piece has a purpose and the account has more than one register.

01 · ORIGIN

Why I’m here.

The motivation behind the business, ambition, and the personal context that helps an audience understand the person speaking.

02 · JUDGMENT

Standards and people.

Observations about respect, boundaries, authority, and how people behave. A direct opening makes the idea clear immediately.

03 · EXPERIENCE

What business taught me.

Lessons about hiring, conflict, decision-making, and ownership. Each script is organized around one idea that can carry a short video.

04 · DELIVERY

A practical filming brief.

Hooks, scripts, captions, and filming direction turn the editorial plan into material the founder can record.

FLP’s role

Personal-brand content strategy, editorial themes, short-form script development, captions, and creative direction. The playbook gives the work a shared voice while leaving room for the founder’s own experience and delivery.

What the work shows

A defined set of themes and a repeatable path from an observation to a script and a social asset. The visual example pairs a direct statement with a restrained setting, giving the words room to lead.

This case study documents the content approach and creative work. It does not attribute follower growth, leads, or revenue to a particular post or campaign.
