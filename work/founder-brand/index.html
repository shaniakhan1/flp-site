OUR FOUNDER’S BUSINESS · SEPTEMBER 2026

An audience is the beginning. Build somewhere for it to go.

Shania Khan’s personal brand connects social storytelling with newsletters, books, and Soft60. It is a working example of the thinking she brings to FLP: give people a reason to listen, then a meaningful next step.

The published audience

  • 481K+ combined community across the founder’s platforms
  • 228K Instagram followers
  • 206K TikTok followers

Source: Shania Khan’s 2026 media kit, checked September 29, 2026. These are self-reported audience snapshots, not audited growth or revenue measurements. Combined counts can include the same person on more than one platform. This is the founder’s own business, not a client campaign or a guarantee of results.

The business problem

A post can get attention and still leave people unsure what to do next. Shania’s work extends beyond individual posts into an identifiable point of view and a range of ways to stay connected.

A voice people can recognize

Rich Grandma Energy, Soft Power, and the idea that luxury is nervous system regulation give the content a recognizable perspective. The language comes from the founder’s own ideas. That is the starting point for FLP’s brand work: understand what a business actually believes before deciding what it should publish.

Content with a next step

Social channels introduce the ideas. Newsletters allow longer conversations. Books and Soft60 give people a way to engage beyond the feed. Shania has also built email and sales funnels as part of her own business. These are different parts of the customer journey, with different jobs.

For a client, the next step may be a booking, a qualified inquiry, or joining an email list. The right choice depends on what the business sells and how its customers buy.

What this demonstrates

The public audience figures document reach in the founder’s own ecosystem. The broader business shows how content, a distinct voice, and owned offers can fit together. This page does not attribute sales to any specific channel or claim a client growth rate.

Apply the thinking to your business
