Know what happens next.

Good work needs a clear beginning. Here is how we organize a social media engagement, from your first inquiry to the first performance review.

Social media management starts at $4,000/month. We confirm your channels, content count, formats, revision rounds, production needs, and total price in writing before you commit.

Before the first month

We review fit and availability, agree on the scope, and organize the agreement and billing. Your start date depends on the agreed schedule and when the required access and assets are ready.

WEEK 1 · UNDERSTAND

Your business, in your words.

We review your audience, offer, existing channels, voice, and priorities. You name one approval contact and provide approved brand materials and secure account access.

WEEK 2 · PLAN

Give the content a direction.

We develop content themes and the first agreed batch. You receive a plan showing what each piece is meant to communicate and where it will appear.

WEEK 3 · REFINE

Review it together.

Your team sends consolidated feedback. We make the revisions included in your scope, confirm final approval, and schedule approved assets.

WEEK 4 · LEARN

See what to carry forward.

We review the available early results and production process, then use what we learned to shape the next batch. A first month is a starting point, not a promise of a specific growth rate.

This is an illustrative sequence. Your written scope sets the actual timeline. Access delays, late feedback, or missing footage can move publishing dates.

What we need from you

  • One decision-maker for approvals and consolidated feedback
  • Accurate product, service, location, and offer information
  • Brand assets and the photography or footage agreed in the scope
  • Account invitations through the platform’s access tools, rather than passwords in a form

What needs a separate scope?

Original filming, travel, paid advertising, creator fees, PR campaigns, email marketing, and funnel builds. Community management and extra channels must be explicitly included in your proposal.
